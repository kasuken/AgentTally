Methodology and limits

Based on CarbonBit methodology v1.1.0. AgentTally uses reported tokens only; it never infers missing usage from text or projects an annual footprint.

Factors and source notes

CarbonBit anchors inference energy to Epoch AI’s 2025 estimate for a typical ChatGPT query. Reference points include Google’s 2025 study of AI serving and Ember’s Global Electricity Review 2024. Actual hardware, batching, location and time are unknown.

Everyday comparisons use CarbonBit’s v1.1.0 reference amounts: household devices compare energy, UK transport factors compare emissions, and household water uses compare volume. They illustrate scale, not resources saved or activities actually performed.

Includes data-center overhead. Excludes training, hardware manufacture, your device and water used to generate electricity. Estimates are rounded; displayed parts may not add up to the displayed total.
